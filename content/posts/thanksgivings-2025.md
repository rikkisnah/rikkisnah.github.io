> Sharmilla and Georges flew far from the cold, <br/>
> From Mauritius to Canada, and across oceans bold. <br/>
> With Isaiah beside them, smiling and bright, <br/>
> They landed in Seattle for warmth, food, and light. 

> Rikesh and Estelle opened their home with grace, <br/>
> Where Rekha and Roy brought joy to the space. <br/>
> Cousins united, a family made whole;  <br/>
> Laughter and stories that strengthen the soul. 

> We celebrated Isaiah; his birthday, his cheer, <br/>
> A memory stamped into this beautiful year. <br/>
> Then Black Friday came with its colorful storm, <br/>
> Crowds and bright lights becoming our norm.

> Isaiah learned something deeper that day; <br/>
> What the U.S. Army stands for, its proud, steady way.<br/>
> With an Army cap on, he carried that pride, <br/>
> A motto of honor he'll keep by his side.

> From Thanksgiving dinner to moments we keep,<br/>
> From late-night jokes to promises deep,<br/>
> This visit reminded us; family is gold, <br/>
> A treasure that warms us in ways untold. <br/>
> Until the next gathering, may the memories stay, <br/>
> Of a Thanksgiving shared in the most special way.
