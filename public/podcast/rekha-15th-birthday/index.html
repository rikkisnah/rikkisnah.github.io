Show Notes

A special birthday message for Rekha Laxmi Clara Kisnah on her 15th birthday. This episode features a poem written for her, celebrating the princess who turned a house into a kingdom.

The Birthday Poem

Fifteen suns have risen for you, Rekha— The princess who turned a house into a kingdom. A learner, a dreamer, a spark that won’t dim, You now stand at the edge of new roads— Eligible to drive, but already steering hearts.

Your Dada once dreamt you’d be a lawyer, And Chouma (Doris) at Harvard would employ her— Books in hand, wisdom in your eyes. Your dad was the first to kiss your forehead, A secret your mom pretends not to know.

When you’re sad, clouds gather and rain; When you smile, the whole sky clears. You drive your brother wild, your parents too— But they’d never trade a second of you.

Stay bold, stay kind, stay you, Rekha. The world will know your name. Happy birthday, center of our galaxy.
